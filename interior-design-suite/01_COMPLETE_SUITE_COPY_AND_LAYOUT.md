## 🎨 DESIGN SYSTEM & AESTHETIC GUIDELINES

### 1. Aesthetic Direction: "Warm Minimalist Editorial" (Japandi / Organic Modern)
Interior designers have hyper-sensitive aesthetic standards. They do not want generic colorful shapes. They want layouts that feel like *Architectural Digest*, *Cereal Magazine*, or *Kinfolk*.

### 2. Verified Color Palette (Hex Codes)
* **Background Primary (Linen Alabaster):** `#FAF8F5` (Soft warm white, never pure `#FFFFFF`)
* **Background Accent (Oatmeal Cream):** `#F3EFEA` (Warm card containers, sidebars)
* **Text Primary (Charcoal Noir):** `#1E1D1B` (Deep soft charcoal, never harsh `#000000`)
* **Text Secondary (Warm Stone):** `#6B665F` (Subtitles, body copy, page numbers)
* **Accent Tone 1 (Muted Olive / Sage):** `#7C8271` (Badges, process lines, highlights)
* **Accent Tone 2 (Warm Terracotta / Sienna):** `#B88069` (Optional warm accent)
* **Dividers & Borders (Soft Bone):** `#E5DFD7` (Subtle 0.5pt to 1pt lines)

### 3. Font Pairings (Available Free in Canva & Google Fonts)
* **Primary Header / Titles:** `Cormorant Garamond` (Light or Medium, all caps with wide letter-spacing, or italicized for editorial accents). Alternative: `Playfair Display` or `Cinzel`.
* **Sub-Headers / Section Labels:** `Montserrat` (Light / Medium, All Caps, Letter Spacing: 150–200).
* **Body Copy:** `Inter` or `Aileron` or `Lora` (Size 9pt–10.5pt, Line Spacing 1.4–1.5, Left-aligned with comfortable margins).
