Part 2 • Client Onboarding Revision Parameters
SCOPE INTEGRITY

Revisions & Scope Changes

Protecting your construction timeline and eliminating unexpected expenses.

Included Revisions

Your contract includes two (2) complimentary rounds of revisions during the Concept Phase.

Permitted Adjustments: Swapping a dining chair, selecting an alternative fabric, or fine-tuning paint shades.

What Is a Scope Change?

Substantial additions outside the agreed initial Letter of Agreement.

Examples: Adding extra rooms, pivoting design styles entirely, or altering architectural layouts after construction sign-off.

Change Order Protocol

Any scope changes require a written Change Order signed by both parties. Additional hours are billed at our standard studio rate of [$150/hr].

Part 2 • Client Onboarding Client FAQ
RESOLVING DOUBTS

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the most common questions regarding purchases and warranties.

Can I buy furniture pieces myself?

To ensure spatial scale accuracy, durability, and warranty protections, we require managing all core furnishings. Cherished heirlooms will be integrated during the concept phase.

What if an item is backordered?

Global supply chains fluctuate. If an item is delayed, we present two designer-approved alternatives of equal quality to maintain project momentum.

What if something arrives damaged?

Our warehouse inspects freight on delivery and manages claims directly with the manufacturer. You never have to deal with shipping disputes.
