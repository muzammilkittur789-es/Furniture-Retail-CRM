## 📌 PINTEREST ALGORITHM & SEO RULES
1. **Pinterest is a Search Engine, Not Social Media:** Pins take 30 to 90 days to gain algorithmic traction and will drive clicks for 6 to 18 months.
2. **Fresh Pins Rule:** Do not pin the exact same image over and over. Use **distinct visual designs** pointing to the same product URL.
3. **Keyword Stuffing vs. Natural SEO:** Include your primary keyword in the **Pin Title**, the **first sentence of the Description**, and as **Text on the Pin Image**.
